Frequently asked questions

How much does membership cost?

The joining fee is shown on the membership form. Renewal is R100 per year.

How do I renew my membership?

Open the renewal form, enter your member number and the email you joined with, pay R100 and upload your proof of payment. An officer confirms the payment and extends your membership by 12 months.

How do I pay and show proof of payment?

Pay using the details on the form, then upload a photo or PDF of the receipt or bank slip.

What happens after I apply?

Your branch officer confirms payment and endorses the application, then the continental administrators give final approval. You receive your member number afterwards.

I lost my member number. What do I do?

Send us a message below with your full name and the email you applied with.
