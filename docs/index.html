Who we are

A regional voice for Muslim women and girls

The Southern Africa Muslimah Network (SAMN) is a non-profit, non-political, faith-based regional network. Guided by the Qur’an and Sunnah, SAMN works to foster regional solidarity, advance the rights and empowerment of Muslim women and girls, and promote their meaningful participation in society.

01

Our Vision

A united Southern Africa where Muslim women thrive through faith, empowerment, and collective action.

02

Our Mission

To empower Muslim women and girls by promoting Islamic values, advancing education and economic opportunity, and advocating for rights, health, and regional solidarity.

03

Our Core Values

Integrity, Respect, Transparency, Faithfulness, Confidentiality and Trustworthiness.

Our constitutional mandate

Nine objectives guiding our regional work

SAMN’s continental work is organised around the nine objectives set out in Article IV of the 2026 Constitution.

OBJECTIVE 01Unified Islamic Scholarship and Da’wah

Advance Qur’an and Sunnah education, scholarly exchanges and context-aware Da’wah.

OBJECTIVE 02Women’s Empowerment Frameworks

Advance regional education, leadership, economic empowerment and cross-border mentorship.

OBJECTIVE 03Women’s Rights and Health Policies

Advocate for the rights, health and wellbeing of Muslim women.

OBJECTIVE 04Pan-African Solidarity and Civic Leadership

Strengthen civic participation, community development and collective action.

OBJECTIVE 05Islamic Cultural Heritage in Africa

Document and celebrate diverse African Muslim heritage and intercultural dialogue.

OBJECTIVE 06Youth Leadership and Innovation

Develop young Muslim women through leadership, innovation and intergenerational dialogue.

OBJECTIVE 07Strategic Policy Influence

Develop research, position papers and advocacy on issues affecting women and families.

OBJECTIVE 08Humanitarian Response and Social Protection

Coordinate humanitarian support, psychosocial networks and crisis response.

OBJECTIVE 09Multilateral and Global Partnerships

Build collaborations with Islamic bodies, women’s networks and development partners.

Governance & accountability

Guided by our Constitution

SAMN’s governance, membership, decision-making, financial accountability and standards of conduct are governed by the SAMN Constitution 2026 and its schedules.

  • Continental Executive Committee (EXCO)
  • Board of Trustees (Founders)
  • Standing committees established under Article IX
  • Recognised Country Branches under Schedule II
  • Code of Conduct and dispute resolution framework
“This Constitution is our covenant to work collectively toward a future where Muslim women thrive and contribute fully to a prosperous and harmonious region.”

Knowledge & learning

SAMN Online Notes 2026

A compilation of handwritten notes from SAMN Sisters’ online seminars and pre-conference sessions. Each chapter should retain its contributor or presenter credit.

PART I • CHAPTERS 1–16

Pre-SAMN Conference Topics

Unity, time, guest etiquette, Salaah, Hijab, sisterhood, Da’wah and leadership.

PART II • CHAPTERS 17–18

Xenophobia Omnibus

Social psychology, geopolitics, xenophobia and Islamophobia.

PART III • CHAPTERS 19–22

SAMN Business Crunch

Halal business, teamwork, personal branding and money management.

PART IV • CHAPTERS 23–26

SALA SAMN Feedback Sessions

Zakah, project management, public speaking and personal branding.

PART V • CHAPTERS 27–31

Marriage, Faith & Family

Nikah, civil marriage, family matters, wellbeing and child welfare.

Important note: The Marriage, Faith & Family chapters are discussion notes, not final fatwas or legal rulings. Religious rulings should be confirmed with a qualified scholar, legal questions with a family-law professional in the relevant country, and anyone in danger should seek immediate local support.
